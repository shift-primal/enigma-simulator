    - Keyboard Input: A
    - Rotors Position: AAA
    - Plugboard: None

        - Wheel 3 Encryption: B
        - Wheel 2 Encryption: J
        - Wheel 1 Encryption: Z
        - Reflector Encryption: T
        - Wheel 1 Encryption: L
        - Wheel 2 Encryption: K
        - Wheel 3 Encryption: U
        - Plugboard Encryption: U
        - Output (Lampboard): U
